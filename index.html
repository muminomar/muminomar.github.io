About Me

Ph.D. in Computer Network and Security from the Department of Computer Engineering at KFUPM, Saudi Arabia

I am a computer engineering researcher specializing in Federated Learning (FL) and AI applications for IoT systems. With a strong foundation in distributed AI, Wireless communication, lightweight trust systems design, and edge/cloud computing, I now focus on developing trusted distributed systems for resource-constrained environments.

My Ph.D. research at KFUPM centers on "A Multi-Layer Trusted Federated Learning System For Heterogeneous IoT Environment", where I design frameworks that balance:

  • Privacy preservation in distributed AI
  • Resource optimization for edge devices
  • Trust mechanisms for heterogeneous networks
  • Robustness against adversarial attacks
